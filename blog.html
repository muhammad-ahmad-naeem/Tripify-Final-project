Essential Pakistan Travel Tips

🛂 Visa & Documentation

Most nationalities can get visa on arrival or e-visa. Keep copies of your passport and visa separately.

Official Visa Portal →

💵 Currency & Budget

Pakistani Rupee (PKR) is the currency. Budget travelers can manage with $30-50 per day comfortably.

Currency Converter →

🌤️ Best Time to Visit

March-May and September-November offer the best weather for exploring most regions of Pakistan.

🚗 Transportation

Domestic flights connect major cities. For northern areas, 4x4 vehicles are recommended for mountain roads.
